# Communication Management Plan

## Project Title

**Evaluating Collaboration Tools for Effective Virtual IT Project Management: A Comparative Study of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack**

## 1. Purpose

The Communication Management Plan defines how project information will be created, shared, stored and communicated throughout the project.

Effective communication is particularly important because the project focuses on virtual IT project management and collaboration tools.

The plan aims to ensure that stakeholders receive appropriate information at the right time and through an appropriate communication channel.

---

# 2. Communication Objectives

The project communication objectives are to:

1. Ensure stakeholders receive relevant project information.
2. Maintain regular communication with the Academic Supervisor.
3. Record important project decisions and changes.
4. Communicate research requirements clearly to participants.
5. Maintain confidentiality when communicating research information.
6. Provide regular project progress updates.
7. Ensure project documentation is accessible and organised.
8. Support effective decision-making throughout the project.

# 4. Communication Channels

## GitHub

GitHub will be used as the central platform for storing and version-controlling project documentation and project artefacts.

Examples include:

* Project Charter
* Business Case
* Stakeholder Register
* WBS
* Project Schedule
* Risk Register
* RACI Matrix
* Communication Plan
* Research documentation
* Data-analysis scripts
* Final project artefacts

## Email

Email will be used for formal communication with:

* Academic Supervisor
* Research participants where appropriate
* IT professionals
* Other relevant stakeholders

## Online Meetings

Online meetings may be conducted with the Academic Supervisor or research participants where appropriate.

Potential platforms include:

* Microsoft Teams
* Zoom
* Google Meet

The selected platform will depend on stakeholder availability and institutional requirements.

## Questionnaire Platform

An online survey platform will be used to collect questionnaire responses.

The selected platform will depend on:

* University requirements
* Data-protection requirements
* Accessibility
* Available functionality

# 6. Project Status Reporting

Project status will be monitored using the following categories:

### Green — On Track

The project is progressing according to the approved schedule and no significant issues have been identified.

### Amber — At Risk

There is a potential issue that may affect schedule, scope, quality or research outcomes.

A mitigation action should be identified.

### Red — Off Track

A significant issue is affecting project delivery and corrective action or escalation is required.

---

# 7. Communication Content

Regular project updates should include:

* Work completed
* Work planned
* Current project phase
* Upcoming milestones
* Schedule status
* Current risks
* Issues requiring attention
* Decisions made
* Changes to project scope
* Research progress

---

# 8. Meeting Management

Where project meetings are conducted, the following process will be used:

### Before the Meeting

* Define meeting objectives.
* Prepare an agenda.
* Identify required participants.
* Prepare relevant documents.

### During the Meeting

* Record key discussions.
* Record decisions.
* Identify actions.
* Assign action owners.
* Agree deadlines where required.

### After the Meeting

* Update project documentation.
* Record decisions.
* Track actions.
* Communicate relevant information to stakeholders.

---

# 9. Research Participant Communication

Research participants will receive clear information about:

* Purpose of the research
* What participation involves
* Expected time commitment
* Voluntary nature of participation
* Data handling
* Confidentiality where applicable
* Withdrawal arrangements where applicable
* Contact information for research questions

Participants should not be pressured to participate.

---

# 10. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Project communication will follow applicable university requirements and relevant data-protection principles.

The project will:

* Collect only necessary information.
* Avoid unnecessary personal data.
* Store research data securely.
* Anonymise or pseudonymise data where appropriate.
* Restrict access to research data.
* Avoid publishing identifiable participant information without appropriate permission.
* Follow the university's research ethics and data-protection requirements.

---

# 11. Communication Escalation

Communication issues will be escalated according to their potential impact.

| Issue Level | Example                                     | Action                                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low         | Minor delay or clarification                | Resolve directly                                                       |
| Medium      | Potential schedule or research issue        | Discuss with supervisor                                                |
| High        | Significant project risk or ethical concern | Escalate promptly to supervisor / university according to requirements |

---

# 12. Communication Records

Important project communications and decisions will be documented where appropriate.

Project records may include:

* Meeting notes
* Decisions
* Action lists
* Email correspondence
* Project updates
* Change records
* Risk updates

Relevant project documentation will be stored in the project repository where appropriate and where doing so does not expose confidential or personal information.

---

# 13. Communication Review

The Communication Management Plan will be reviewed periodically to ensure that:

* Stakeholders receive appropriate information.
* Communication frequency remains suitable.
* Communication channels remain effective.
* Research participants receive clear information.
* Confidentiality requirements are maintained.
* Communication issues are addressed promptly.
