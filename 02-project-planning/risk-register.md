# Risk Register

## Project Title

**Evaluating Collaboration Tools for Effective Virtual IT Project Management: A Comparative Study of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack**

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Risk Register is to identify, assess, monitor and manage risks that could affect the successful delivery of the project.

Risks will be reviewed regularly throughout the project and updated when new risks are identified or existing risks change.

## 2. Risk Rating Method

Risk exposure is assessed using:

**Risk Score = Probability × Impact**

### Probability

| Score | Rating    | Description             |
| ----: | --------- | ----------------------- |
|     1 | Very Low  | Unlikely to occur       |
|     2 | Low       | Unlikely but possible   |
|     3 | Medium    | Possible                |
|     4 | High      | Likely to occur         |
|     5 | Very High | Almost certain to occur |

### Impact

| Score | Rating    | Description             |
| ----: | --------- | ----------------------- |
|     1 | Very Low  | Minimal effect          |
|     2 | Low       | Minor effect            |
|     3 | Medium    | Noticeable effect       |
|     4 | High      | Significant effect      |
|     5 | Very High | Major effect on project |

### Risk Score

| Score | Level    | Response                   |
| ----: | -------- | -------------------------- |
|   1–4 | Low      | Monitor                    |
|   5–9 | Medium   | Mitigate                   |
| 10–16 | High     | Active management required |
| 17–25 | Critical | Immediate action required  |

# 4. Top Risks

The highest-priority risks currently identified are:

### R01 — Difficulty Recruiting Participants

**Score: 15 — High**

The project requires primary research data. Failure to recruit sufficient participants could reduce the quality and usefulness of the research findings.

**Mitigation:**

* Recruit early.
* Use university networks.
* Contact IT professionals.
* Use professional networks where appropriate.
* Clearly communicate the purpose and time commitment.

---

### R02 — Insufficient Survey Responses

**Score: 15 — High**

A low response rate could reduce the reliability of quantitative analysis.

**Mitigation:**

* Pilot the questionnaire.
* Keep the questionnaire concise.
* Distribute it through multiple appropriate channels.
* Monitor response numbers weekly.

---

### R05 — Scope Creep

**Score: 12 — High**

The project could become too broad if additional collaboration tools, research questions or evaluation criteria are continuously added.

**Mitigation:**

The project will initially remain focused on:

* Jira
* Microsoft Teams
* Slack

Any significant change to scope will be documented and assessed before being accepted.

---

### R15 — Insufficient Time for Final Report

**Score: 15 — High**

Leaving report writing until the final weeks could create significant schedule pressure.

**Mitigation:**

Report sections will be developed progressively throughout the project rather than being written entirely at the end.

---

# 5. Risk Monitoring Process

The Risk Register will be reviewed at least once per week.

During each review, the Project Manager will:

1. Review existing risks.
2. Identify new risks.
3. Update probability and impact scores.
4. Review mitigation actions.
5. Update risk status.
6. Identify risks requiring escalation.
7. Record significant changes.

## Risk Status

The following status categories will be used:

* **Open** — Risk has been identified and is being monitored.
* **Mitigating** — Actions are currently being taken.
* **Closed** — Risk is no longer relevant or has been successfully addressed.
* **Occurred** — The risk has become an actual project issue.

## Risk Ownership

The Project Manager is initially responsible for monitoring all project risks. If additional project team members are introduced, risk ownership may be reassigned based on responsibility and expertise.
