# Stakeholder Register

## Project Title

**Evaluating Collaboration Tools for Effective Virtual IT Project Management: A Comparative Study of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack**

## 1. Purpose

The stakeholder register identifies individuals and groups who may influence the project, contribute to the research, or be affected by the project's findings.

Stakeholders will be analysed according to their level of interest and influence so that appropriate communication and engagement strategies can be developed.

## 3. Stakeholder Analysis

### High Influence / High Interest

**Project Manager**

The Project Manager has direct responsibility for planning and delivering the project.

**Academic Supervisor**

The Academic Supervisor has significant influence over the academic quality, methodology and successful completion of the project.

### High Influence / Medium Interest

**University**

The University establishes academic, ethical and research requirements that must be followed.

### Medium Influence / High Interest

**Research Participants**

Participants provide important primary research data and therefore have a significant interest in the study.

**IT Project Managers**

IT project managers can provide professional insight into the practical use of collaboration tools.

**Scrum Masters**

Scrum Masters may provide valuable information about collaboration, communication and task-management practices in Agile teams.

**Virtual IT Team Members**

Remote team members can provide practical experience of using collaboration tools.

### Medium Influence / Medium Interest

**Software Developers**

Developers are potential users of collaboration tools and can provide technical perspectives.

**Business Analysts**

Business analysts may provide information about requirements, communication and stakeholder collaboration.

### Low Influence / Low Interest

**Collaboration Tool Vendors**

Vendors provide the platforms being evaluated but have limited influence over the academic research project.

## 4. Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

### Manage Closely

Stakeholders with high influence and high interest will receive regular communication.

Activities include:

* Progress meetings
* Project updates
* Review of major deliverables
* Feedback sessions
* Issue escalation where required

### Keep Satisfied

High-influence stakeholders with lower day-to-day involvement will be provided with relevant project information and compliance updates.

### Keep Informed

Research participants and other interested stakeholders will receive clear information about the purpose of the study, participation requirements and relevant outcomes.

### Monitor

Low-influence and low-interest stakeholders will be monitored without requiring regular direct engagement.

## 6. Stakeholder Management Principles

The project will:

* Respect participant privacy.
* Provide clear research information.
* Obtain appropriate consent before collecting primary data.
* Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
* Maintain confidentiality where required.
* Communicate project changes appropriately.
* Record important stakeholder feedback.
* Review stakeholder needs throughout the project.
