# Research Questionnaire

## Project Title

**Evaluating Collaboration Tools for Effective Virtual IT Project Management: A Comparative Study of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack**

---

# Participant Information

## Purpose of the Study

This questionnaire is part of an academic research project investigating the effectiveness of collaboration tools in virtual IT project management.

The study focuses on:

* Jira
* Microsoft Teams
* Slack

The research aims to understand how these tools support communication, task management, collaboration, information sharing, project visibility and productivity in virtual or hybrid IT project environments.

Participation is voluntary.

The researcher will confirm the relevant university research-ethics and consent requirements before collecting research responses.

* Project visibility
* Productivity

The Evaluation Framework weights will be applied to the tool scores.

* Frequency

# 8. Data Quality Rules

Before analysis:

1. Invalid consent responses will be excluded according to the approved research procedure.
2. Duplicate responses will be checked.
3. Missing responses will be documented.
4. Tool ratings will only be analysed where participants have relevant experience.
5. Data-cleaning decisions will be documented.
6. No data will be fabricated.
7. No responses will be changed simply to produce a preferred result.
