# Research Questions

## Project Title

**Evaluating Collaboration Tools for Effective Virtual IT Project Management: A Comparative Study of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack**

---

## 1. Research Aim

The aim of this research is to evaluate and compare selected collaboration tools and determine how effectively they support virtual IT project management.

The study focuses on Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack.

---

# 2. Main Research Question

### RQ1

**How effectively do collaboration tools support virtual IT project management?**

This question provides the overall direction for the research.

The study will investigate how collaboration tools support communication, task management, information sharing, coordination and productivity within virtual IT project environments.

---

# 3. Supporting Research Questions

### RQ2

**What collaboration requirements are considered important by people working in virtual IT project environments?**

This question will identify the features and capabilities that users consider important when working remotely or in hybrid IT project teams.

Areas may include:

* Communication
* Task management
* File and information sharing
* Team coordination
* Project visibility
* Notifications
* Integration
* Ease of use

---

### RQ3

**How do Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack support key virtual IT project-management activities?**

This question will be addressed through a structured comparison of the three selected tools.

The comparison will consider:

* Communication
* Task management
* Collaboration
* Information sharing
* Usability
* Integration
* Project visibility
* Productivity

---

### RQ4

**How do users perceive the effectiveness and usability of Jira, Microsoft Teams and Slack?**

This question will be investigated using questionnaire responses and, where possible, interviews.

Participants may be asked to rate tools based on:

* Ease of use
* Communication effectiveness
* Collaboration effectiveness
* Task-management effectiveness
* Information accessibility
* Overall usefulness

---

### RQ5

**What challenges do virtual IT project teams experience when using collaboration tools?**

This question will explore potential problems such as:

* Information overload
* Notification overload
* Communication difficulties
* Lack of integration
* Poor adoption
* Learning difficulties
* Fragmented information
* Difficulty tracking project work
* Tool limitations

---

### RQ6

**Which collaboration tool, or combination of tools, is most appropriate for different virtual IT project requirements?**

The research will use the collected evidence to identify situations where one tool may be more appropriate than another.

The research will not assume that one tool is universally superior.

Instead, recommendations will be based on:

* User responses
* Evaluation scores
* Literature findings
* Tool capabilities
* Project-management requirements

# 6. Research Variables

The questionnaire will measure several factors related to collaboration-tool effectiveness.

## Independent / Evaluation Factors

Potential factors include:

* Type of IT role
* Frequency of tool usage
* Virtual/hybrid working experience

## Dependent / Outcome Measures

Potential outcome measures include:

* Perceived effectiveness
* Ease of use
* Communication effectiveness
* Collaboration effectiveness
* Task-management effectiveness
* Information-sharing effectiveness
* Project visibility
* Perceived productivity

---

# 7. Proposed Evaluation Scale

Where appropriate, questionnaire questions will use a five-point Likert scale:

| Score | Response                   |
| ----: | -------------------------- |
|     1 | Strongly Disagree          |
|     2 | Disagree                   |
|     3 | Neither Agree nor Disagree |
|     4 | Agree                      |
|     5 | Strongly Agree             |

For tool-rating questions, a five-point scale may also be used:

| Score | Rating    |
| ----: | --------- |
|     1 | Very Poor |
|     2 | Poor      |
|     3 | Average   |
|     4 | Good      |
|     5 | Excellent |

The final questionnaire will clearly distinguish between agreement scales and performance/rating scales to avoid participant confusion.

---

# 8. Expected Relationship Between Research Questions and Analysis

The research questions will guide the data-analysis process.

### RQ2

Participant responses will be analysed to identify which collaboration features are considered most important.

### RQ3

The three tools will be compared using the defined evaluation framework.

### RQ4

User ratings will be analysed using descriptive statistics such as:

* Mean
* Median
* Frequency
* Percentage

### RQ5

Open-ended responses and interviews will be analysed to identify recurring themes and challenges.

### RQ6

The findings from the different research activities will be combined to develop evidence-based recommendations.
